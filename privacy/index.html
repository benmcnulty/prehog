Privacy on /prehog

This one page runs analytics; the rest of benlive.tv deliberately doesn't. Here's exactly what that means.

Why this page is different

benlive.tv/prehog is a working PostHog implementation, built to demonstrate PostHog competence for a job application — so it's the one place on this site instrumented with the PostHog JS SDK. Everywhere else, including AI Lab, stays analytics-free. Scoping it this way keeps that "no analytics" claim honest elsewhere, and keeps the blast radius of adding a third-party script contained to the one page that's explicitly about it.

What's collected, and why

Page views
PostHog's standard $pageview autocapture, fired once on load. Answers "did anyone open the page."
Slide progress
Nine small events tied to the deck's own nine sections — which slide is active, how you navigated (click, arrow key, swipe, dot), whether you reached the end, whether the auto-advance timer is playing or paused, whether you opened the "what does this page measure" panel, and which evidence links you clicked through to. Each one exists to answer a specific, stated question about whether the presentation itself works — not to build a profile. The full list, with the exact question each answers, is in this repo's own analytics.md.
Session Replay
Turned on for this page only, with maskAllInputs: true. It exists to answer one specific question — is the slide navigation discoverable on a phone — and is meant to come off once that's answered, not run indefinitely by default.
A short survey
Shown once, after you reach the final slide: a 1–5 rating and one optional open-text question. The open-text answer is the only free text this page ever collects, and it's masked in Session Replay even though the response itself is recorded as normal survey data.
One feature flag
Controls whether a live, self-referential event-log panel is visible to a given visitor — a real rollout control, not a fake A/B test with no branch to measure.
JavaScript errors
Unhandled exceptions and unhandled promise rejections only, so a bug that only shows up on someone's actual phone in production doesn't go unnoticed. Nothing else in the console is captured.
One sessionStorage key
prehog:autoplay remembers whether you paused the auto-advancing slideshow, so it doesn't restart if you navigate within the same tab. It never leaves your browser.

What's deliberately not collected

No names, no email addresses, no precise or IP-derived location beyond PostHog's default country-level estimate, and no cross-site identity linking — there's no identify() call anywhere in this page's code, so every visitor stays anonymous to it. The only free text collected is the survey's optional open answer, described above, and only if you choose to type one.

Where it goes

Events are sent directly to PostHog's US cloud (us.i.posthog.com) under my own PostHog project. See PostHog's own privacy policy for how they handle it on their end. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties for advertising, or used for anything beyond understanding whether this presentation works.

Want to see it for yourself?

The "what does this page measure?" panel on slide 6 of the deck shows a live log of every event your own session has actually sent — including your anonymous distinct_id — when enabled. The event taxonomy, the reasoning behind every product turned on or deliberately left off, and the full source are public in the prehog repository.
